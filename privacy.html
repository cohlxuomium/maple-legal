Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Maple is a workout logger with a pet companion and an optional camera coach. This policy explains what Maple keeps, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have. The short version: your training lives on your phone, you can use Maple without an account, and we do not sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Maple stores this on your device: your workouts and sets, body weight and height if you enter them, your goal and setup answers, your settings, your pet and its progress, quests, and streaks.

Deleting the app removes it. Without an account, this data is never sent anywhere.

Camera coach

The camera coach watches your lift to count reps and check form. The video is processed on your phone, inside the app, and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

The pose model is built into the app, so the camera works without internet. Only joint positions (13 points per frame) are used, and only while the coach is open.

Voice logging

When you log a set by voice, your phone's speech recognition turns your speech into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple or Google may process that audio under their own privacy terms. Maple receives the text, not the recording.

If Maple cannot read a phrase on its own, the text of that phrase may be sent to our phrase reading service, with a little context such as your current lift and weight unit, to work out the weight and reps. The same service may also be sent the numbers of a set (weight, reps, range, bar speed, form score) to write the short note after a camera set. No audio, video, name, or account details are sent. Voice is optional: you can log every set by tapping.

Optional account and sync

You can sign in with Apple to save your plan and back up your data. This is optional. If you sign in, Maple stores a copy of your workouts, body weight, profile and setup answers, settings that sync, and your pet's progress on our servers (hosted with Supabase), linked to your account, so you can restore it on another device.

Sign in with Apple shares your Apple account identifier with us, and your email address or Apple's private relay address if you choose to share it.

You can delete your account and the copy on our servers inside the app, from Profile. The data on your phone stays until you remove it.

Purchases

Maple Pro is a subscription handled by the App Store or Google Play. Payment details go to them and are never seen by Maple.

We use Superwall to show the paywall and to know whether a subscription is active. Superwall receives a random app user identifier (or your account identifier if you signed in), your purchase status, and basic device information such as device model and OS version.

Crash reports

Release builds send crash reports through Sentry so we can fix bugs. A report includes the error, app version, device model and OS version. We do not put your workouts, name, or email in it.

Notifications

Reminders and rest timer alerts are scheduled on your phone. They are local notifications, and no push server or token is involved. You can turn them off in Profile or in your phone settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not show ads or track you across other apps and sites.
  • We do not use your workouts or camera to train models.

Your choices

You can use Maple without an account, turn off the pet companion, the camera, voice, sound, and reminders, and delete your account at any time. You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it.

Email us at xualt@icloud.com.

Keeping data

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or the app. Data in your account is kept until you delete the account, and is removed from our servers when you do. Crash reports are kept for a limited time by Sentry.

Fitness information

Body weight, height, and training history are fitness information you choose to enter. Maple is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Children

Maple is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app. Using Maple after a change means you accept it.
