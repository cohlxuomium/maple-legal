How do I delete my account and data?

Open Profile, find the cloud sync card, and choose Delete account. This removes your copy from our servers. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.

How do I cancel Maple Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Maple. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Profile and tap Restore purchases in the Maple Pro card, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

Does the camera record me?

No. The camera is processed on your phone and nothing is recorded or uploaded.
